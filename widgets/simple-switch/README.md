# Simple switch (`hermes.simple-switch`)

The starter widget: one switch or plug, its name, on or off, and a toggle. It is the worked example in
[docs/AUTHORING.md](../../docs/AUTHORING.md); copy this folder to start a widget of your own.

- **Works with:** switches and plugs (`switch`, `plug`).
- **It can:** turn on and off (`on_off.turn_on`, `on_off.turn_off`).
- **Sizes:** Small (default) and Medium (adds the icon, the room when there is one, and when it last reported).
